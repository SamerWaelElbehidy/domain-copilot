# Dawlia Furniture Works — Contractor & Hot-Work Permit Policy

## Overview & Specifications

This policy governs any hot work (welding, grinding, cutting, open
flame) performed by employees or contractors anywhere in the facility,
and any contractor access to production areas generally. It works
alongside the Combustible Dust Management Policy's hot-work
requirement, which applies specifically near dust-generating or
dust-collection equipment.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. No hot work may begin anywhere in the facility without a signed
   hot-work permit issued by a supervisor for that specific date,
   location, and task.
2. A dedicated fire watch, separate from the person performing the hot
   work, is required for the duration of the work and for at least 30
   minutes after it ends.
3. Hot work within 10 meters of any Dust Extraction & Collection System
   DCS-800 ducting or the baghouse requires an additional combustible-
   dust-specific sign-off per the Combustible Dust Management Policy,
   not just the standard hot-work permit.
4. All contractors performing hot work must be escorted per the General
   Workshop Safety Policy's escort requirement and briefed on the
   nearest fire extinguisher location before starting.
5. Any contractor-caused incident or near-miss must be reported per the
   Incident & Near-Miss Reporting Procedure by the escorting employee,
   not left to the contractor alone.
