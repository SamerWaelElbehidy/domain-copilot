# CNC Wood Router DWR-2200 — Maintenance & Operations Manual (Rev. B)

> SUPERSEDED: this revision was replaced by Rev. C on 2025-11-01
> following a near-miss incident involving a partially blocked dust
> port. It is retained in the corpus only as a historical record; do not
> follow this revision's Safety Prerequisites for current operations.

## Overview & Specifications

The DWR-2200 is a 3-axis CNC wood router used at Dawlia Furniture Works'
Damietta facility for cutting, carving, and drilling flat-panel furniture
components (MDF, plywood, solid hardwood up to 60mm). Work envelope:
2200mm x 1300mm x 200mm. Spindle: 9kW air-cooled, 6,000-24,000 RPM.
Dust extraction port: 120mm, must be connected to the shop's central dust
collection system whenever the spindle is running.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Confirm the emergency stop button is unobstructed and tested (press
   and release) before every shift start.
2. Verify the dust extraction system is running and showing normal
   suction pressure on its gauge before starting the spindle. Never run
   the spindle with dust extraction offline.
3. Ensure all four vacuum table zones show full hold-down pressure on the
   control panel before beginning any cut — a workpiece that lifts during
   a high-speed pass is a projectile hazard.
4. Do not open the enclosure door while the spindle is rotating, even if
   the current cut appears finished. Wait for the "SPINDLE STOPPED"
   indicator.
5. Wear hearing protection and a properly fitted dust mask (minimum
   FFP2) at all times during operation.
6. Before any maintenance or blade/bit change, engage the physical
   lockout-tagout switch on the main power panel, not just the on-screen
   stop.

## Installation & Setup

Mount the DWR-2200 on a leveled concrete pad rated for at least 1,800kg.
Connect the 120mm dust port to the shop's dust collection trunk line.
Connect three-phase power (400V, 32A) through a lockable isolator switch
mounted within arm's reach of the operator station. Run the built-in axis
calibration routine before first use.

## Operating Procedures

Load the cut program via USB or the shop network share. Confirm the
material thickness entered in the control panel matches the actual
stock. Start the dust extraction system before starting the spindle.

## Maintenance Schedule

Daily: clear chip tray, wipe down linear rails. Weekly: grease the X/Y
linear bearings, check belt tension. Monthly: inspect spindle collet for
wear, verify vacuum table zone seals. Every 2,000 spindle-hours: full
spindle bearing inspection by a certified technician.

## Diagnostic & Troubleshooting

1. Symptom: spindle overheating during long runs. Likely cause:
   coolant/air flow restricted. Corrective action: check spindle
   air-cooling intake for dust blockage, allow spindle to cool for 30
   minutes before resuming.
2. Symptom: vacuum table failing to hold a zone. Likely cause: a worn
   zone seal, or an undersized workpiece leaving an unsealed gap.
   Corrective action: switch to a smaller matching zone size, inspect
   and replace the zone seal if worn.
