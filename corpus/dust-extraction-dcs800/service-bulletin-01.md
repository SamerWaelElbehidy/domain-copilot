# Service Bulletin SB-DCS-800-01 — Spark Detector Lens Fouling in Humid Conditions

## Overview & Specifications

Applies to all DCS-800 units. Field reports indicate the spark
detection sensor lenses can foul faster than the standard monthly test
interval during Damietta's humid summer months, increasing the risk of
an undetected sensor fault between scheduled tests. This bulletin adds
an interim bi-weekly visual lens check (June-September) on top of the
manual's standard monthly sensor test.

## Diagnostic & Troubleshooting

1. Symptom: spark detection sensor passes its monthly test-tool check
   but the lens shows visible condensation or a thin film during a
   mid-cycle visual inspection. Likely cause: humidity-driven fouling
   between test intervals, which can degrade real-world detection
   sensitivity even when the test tool still registers a pass. Likely
   cause continued: the manufacturer's test tool checks circuit
   function, not real-world optical sensitivity to an actual spark
   under fouled conditions. Corrective action: clean the sensor lens
   per manufacturer procedure at the interim bi-weekly interval during
   humid months; do not treat a passed test-tool check as sufficient
   evidence of a clean lens.
2. Symptom: lens fouling recurs within days of cleaning during peak
   humidity. Likely cause: the standard sensor housing may not be
   adequately sealed for this facility's humidity levels. Corrective
   action: escalate to the manufacturer for an environment-rated sensor
   housing evaluation rather than increasing cleaning frequency
   indefinitely.
