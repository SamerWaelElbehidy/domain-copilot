# Dawlia Furniture Works — Company-wide Lockout-Tagout Standard

## Overview & Specifications

This standard defines the six-step lockout-tagout (LOTO) sequence that
every machine-specific LOTO card in this facility must follow. It does
not replace any individual machine's LOTO card — each machine's card
applies this standard's structure to that machine's specific energy
sources.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Notify all affected personnel before beginning any lockout — no
   isolation step begins without this notification.
2. Shut down the equipment using its documented normal stop sequence,
   never an emergency stop used as a routine shutdown method.
3. Identify and isolate every energy source specific to that machine
   (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, gas, gravitational, or stored
   pressure) — a machine with more than one energy source requires a
   lockout device at every source, not just the primary one.
4. Apply a personal lockout device and a dated, named tag at each
   isolation point before any other step proceeds.
5. Release or block all stored and residual energy at each isolation
   point (bleed pressure, allow residual heat to dissipate, block any
   gravity-loaded component) before any body part enters a hazard zone.
6. Verify isolation with a try-out test — attempt the normal start
   sequence and confirm no motion, heating, or pressure buildup occurs —
   before work begins, and return controls to off immediately after the
   test.
7. Only the person who applied a lockout device may remove it, after
   confirming the work area is clear and all guards are reinstalled.
