# Edge Banding Machine EBM-150 — Lockout/Tagout Card

## Overview & Specifications

This card is the mandatory lockout/tagout (LOTO) sequence for
maintenance on the EBM-150's feed rollers, trim units, or glue pot.
Three isolation points apply: the main electrical disconnect, the
compressed-air supply to the pneumatic hold-down system, and residual
glue pot heat.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Notify affected personnel that the machine is being locked out before
   beginning isolation.
2. Bring the machine to a full stop using the normal control sequence.
3. Open and lock the main electrical disconnect with a personal lockout
   device and dated tag.
4. Close and lock the compressed-air isolation valve, then bleed
   residual air pressure from the pneumatic hold-down lines.
5. Allow the glue pot to cool below 60C before any contact or cleaning
   work, per Safety Prerequisite §6 of the full manual; wear
   heat-resistant gloves if work must begin sooner.
6. Verify isolation with a try-out test at the control panel before
   beginning work near the feed rollers or trim units.
7. On completing work, confirm all guards are reinstalled, notify
   affected personnel, then remove lockout devices in reverse order.
