# Service Bulletin SB-HLP-1200-01 — Light Curtain False-Trips in Dusty Conditions

## Overview & Specifications

Applies to all HLP-1200 units operating near MDF cutting or sanding
stations. Field reports indicate the platen-zone light curtain can
register false trips (stopping the close cycle) when airborne dust
concentration is high, reducing throughput without indicating an actual
fault. This is a nuisance/throughput issue, not a safety issue — the
light curtain must never be adjusted to reduce its sensitivity to
compensate.

## Diagnostic & Troubleshooting

1. Symptom: press repeatedly stops mid-close with no object in the
   platen zone, correlating with visible airborne dust near the
   machine. Likely cause: dust accumulation on the light curtain
   emitter/receiver lenses reducing detection reliability. Corrective
   action: clean the emitter and receiver lenses per the daily
   checklist frequency during high-dust periods, rather than the
   standard weekly interval; do not adjust the light curtain's
   sensitivity setting to reduce trips.
2. Symptom: false trips persist after lens cleaning. Likely cause:
   ambient dust levels at the press location exceed the light curtain's
   rated operating environment. Corrective action: escalate to
   facilities to evaluate relocating the press or improving local dust
   extraction at that station; this is a workplace-environment fix, not
   a light-curtain fault to be worked around at the machine.
