# Service Bulletin SB-KDN-500-01 — Moisture Sensor Pin Connector Corrosion

## Overview & Specifications

Applies to all KDN-500 units. Field reports indicate the moisture-
sensing pin connectors can corrode faster than the standard quarterly
calibration interval in high-humidity operating conditions, producing
readings that drift low (appearing drier than actual) rather than
failing outright. This bulletin adds an interim monthly visual check
in humid-season months (June-October) on top of the manual's standard
quarterly calibration.

## Diagnostic & Troubleshooting

1. Symptom: finished lumber consistently tests wetter with a handheld
   moisture meter than the kiln's own sensor-pin reading at cycle end.
   Likely cause: corroded sensor pin connectors causing a falsely low
   reading, not a drying-performance problem. Corrective action:
   visually inspect pin connectors for green/white corrosion deposits
   before assuming the drying cycle itself underperformed; clean or
   replace corroded connectors before re-running calibration.
2. Symptom: sensor pin reading fluctuates erratically within the same
   sample board during a stable part of the cycle. Likely cause: a
   partially corroded connection with intermittent contact. Corrective
   action: replace the affected connector rather than attempting to
   clean an intermittent contact in place — intermittent faults on this
   connector type do not reliably resolve with cleaning alone.
