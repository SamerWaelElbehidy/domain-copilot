# Dawlia Furniture Works — General Workshop Safety Policy

## Overview & Specifications

This policy applies to all personnel and visitors in the Damietta
production facility, across all equipment. It sets baseline rules that
apply in addition to, not instead of, each machine's own Safety
Prerequisites and Lockout-Tagout Card.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. No one may operate any machine listed in this facility's equipment
   fleet without documented training and sign-off for that specific
   machine.
2. All aisles and emergency exit routes must remain clear of stock,
   pallets, and waste bins at all times — a blocked exit route during an
   evacuation is treated as a serious safety violation, not a
   housekeeping issue.
3. Any near-miss or safety incident must be reported the same shift per
   the Incident & Near-Miss Reporting Procedure, regardless of whether
   it resulted in injury or damage.
4. Personal protective equipment required by a specific machine's manual
   is mandatory, not optional, and supervisors are responsible for
   enforcing it, not merely making it available.
5. No employee may bypass, defeat, or disable a safety interlock,
   guard, or emergency stop on any machine for any reason, including to
   "just finish" a job — a machine with a disabled safety device must be
   tagged out of service immediately.
6. Visitors and contractors must be escorted by a trained employee at
   all times while in production areas and briefed on emergency exit
   routes before entering.
