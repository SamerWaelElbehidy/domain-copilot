# Wood Drying Kiln KDN-500 — Lockout/Tagout Card

## Overview & Specifications

This card is the mandatory lockout/tagout (LOTO) sequence for any
maintenance on the KDN-500 burner, chamber, or control system. Three
isolation points apply: the gas supply shutoff, the main electrical
disconnect, and residual chamber heat.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Notify affected personnel that the kiln is being locked out before
   beginning isolation.
2. Allow any active drying cycle to complete its cool-down phase, or
   manually initiate cool-down from the control panel; do not force an
   immediate stop mid-cycle except in an emergency.
3. Close and lock the dedicated gas shutoff valve outside the burner
   room with a personal lockout device and dated tag.
4. Open and lock the main electrical disconnect with a separate lockout
   device and tag.
5. Confirm chamber internal temperature has dropped below 40C on the
   control panel display before opening any chamber door for
   maintenance, consistent with Safety Prerequisite §4 of the full
   manual.
6. Verify isolation with a try-out test at the control panel (confirm no
   ignition sequence can start) before beginning work.
7. On completing work, confirm all doors and panels are closed, notify
   affected personnel, then remove lockout devices in reverse order
   (electrical first, then gas).
