# Dawlia Furniture Works — Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Standard

## Overview & Specifications

This standard lists baseline PPE required across the facility. Specific
machines may require additional PPE per their own manual's Safety
Prerequisites (for example, the Spray Finishing Booth SFB-300's
solvent-rated respirator cartridge requirement).

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Safety glasses or a full face shield (per task) are required in all
   production areas at all times, no exceptions for brief tasks.
2. Hearing protection is required at any workstation where posted noise
   levels exceed 85dB, including the CNC router and air compressor
   areas.
3. A properly fitted dust mask (minimum FFP2) is required at any
   dust-generating workstation; a respirator with the coating-specific
   cartridge is required in the spray finishing booth, and a dust mask
   is not a substitute for a solvent-rated respirator cartridge.
4. Heat-resistant gloves are required for any task involving contact
   with heated equipment surfaces (kiln fittings, laminating press
   platens, edge bander glue pot) within the cool-down windows specified
   in each machine's manual.
5. Steel-toe safety footwear is required in all production areas at all
   times.
6. Loose clothing, dangling jewelry, and unrestrained long hair are
   prohibited near any machine with exposed moving parts (feed rollers,
   belts, spindles).
