# Service Bulletin SB-SFB-300-01 — Face-Velocity Gauge Calibration Drift

## Overview & Specifications

Applies to all SFB-300 units. Field reports indicate the face-velocity
gauge can drift up to 15% high over 6-9 months of service without any
visible fault indication, meaning the gauge can show an acceptable
reading while true extraction airflow is below the 0.5 m/s minimum
required by Safety Prerequisite §1 of the full manual. This bulletin
adds an interim 6-monthly independent airflow verification on top of
the manual's standard maintenance schedule.

## Diagnostic & Troubleshooting

1. Symptom: face-velocity gauge reads within acceptable range, but
   operators report a persistent solvent odor or haze during normal
   spraying. Likely cause: gauge calibration drift producing a
   falsely-high reading rather than an actual airflow problem — do not
   assume the gauge is correct just because it shows an acceptable
   number. Corrective action: verify true face velocity with an
   independent handheld anemometer at several points across the booth
   opening; if true readings are below 0.5 m/s despite an acceptable
   gauge reading, stop spraying and schedule gauge recalibration
   immediately.
2. Symptom: independent anemometer readings and the installed gauge
   disagree by more than 10%. Likely cause: gauge sensor drift.
   Corrective action: recalibrate or replace the gauge sensor per
   manufacturer procedure; do not resume relying on the installed gauge
   alone until recalibration is confirmed.
