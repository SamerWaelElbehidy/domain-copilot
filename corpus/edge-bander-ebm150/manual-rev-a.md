# Edge Banding Machine EBM-150 — Maintenance & Operations Manual (Rev. A)

## Overview & Specifications

The EBM-150 applies PVC and veneer edge banding to panel furniture
components using a hot-melt adhesive system. Feed speed: 8-18 m/min.
Glue pot operating temperature: 180-210C. Trimming units: top and bottom
flush trim, corner rounding. Feed system: powered rollers with
pneumatic hold-down.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Confirm the glue pot guard is closed and latched before powering on
   the heating element — the glue pot reaches temperatures capable of
   severe burns on contact.
2. Keep hands clear of the feed roller nip points at all times once the
   feed system is powered; use the push-block for any panel shorter than
   400mm.
3. Verify the trim unit blade guards are in place and the interlock
   prevents blade rotation with any guard open.
4. Allow the glue pot to reach full operating temperature and stabilize
   (per the ready indicator) before feeding panels — an underheated glue
   pot causes both weak bonds and glue-strand spatter hazards.
5. Do not reach into the machine to clear a jam without first pressing
   the emergency stop and confirming all motion has ceased.
6. Wear heat-resistant gloves when performing any glue pot cleaning or
   maintenance, even after the "cooling" indicator shows, until the pot
   temperature reads below 60C on the display.

## Installation & Setup

Level the EBM-150 on the shop floor per the manufacturer's leveling
procedure; an unlevel machine causes inconsistent glue-line thickness
across a panel run. Connect compressed air (minimum 6 bar) for the
pneumatic hold-down system. Connect three-phase power through a lockable
isolator positioned within reach of the operator station. Run the
factory calibration routine for feed speed and glue application rate
before first production use.

## Operating Procedures

Select the correct glue pot temperature and feed speed for the banding
material (PVC vs. veneer) per the material chart on the control panel.
Confirm panel edges are clean and free of dust before feeding, as
contamination is the leading cause of poor bond quality. Feed panels
squarely against the guide fence; do not attempt to correct panel
alignment once the feed rollers have engaged. Inspect the first panel of
each run for bond quality and trim finish before continuing the batch.

## Maintenance Schedule

Daily: clean glue residue from the pot rim and applicator roller, check
feed roller surfaces for adhesive buildup. Weekly: inspect trim blade
sharpness, lubricate feed chain per the lubrication chart. Monthly:
inspect pneumatic hold-down cylinders for air leaks, calibrate glue
application rate. Every 500 operating hours: full trim unit bearing
inspection by a certified technician.

## Diagnostic & Troubleshooting

1. Symptom: banding delaminates from the panel edge shortly after
   trimming. Likely cause: glue pot temperature below the material's
   required range, or panel edge contamination. Corrective action:
   verify glue pot temperature against the material chart, inspect
   panel edge cleanliness before the next run; do not increase feed
   speed to compensate for a cold glue pot.
2. Symptom: glue spatter/stringing visible on the finished edge. Likely
   cause: glue pot overheated above the material's range, or applicator
   roller wear. Corrective action: check glue pot temperature setting,
   inspect applicator roller surface for grooving or wear per the
   monthly checklist.
3. Symptom: trim finish shows chatter marks or an uneven edge. Likely
   cause: dull trim blade, or feed speed too high for the material.
   Corrective action: inspect blade sharpness against the wear gauge,
   reduce feed speed for the material type per the operating chart.
4. Symptom: panel jams at the feed roller entry. Likely cause: panel not
   square against the guide fence, or debris built up at the roller
   nip. Corrective action: press emergency stop per Safety Prerequisite
   §5 before clearing; inspect roller nip for debris before resuming
   feed.
