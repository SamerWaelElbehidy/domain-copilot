# Dust Extraction & Collection System DCS-800 — Lockout/Tagout Card

## Overview & Specifications

This card is the mandatory lockout/tagout (LOTO) sequence for
maintenance on the DCS-800 baghouse, hopper, or ducting, and is required
before any hopper emptying beyond routine operation. Two isolation
points apply: the main electrical disconnect (fans and hopper auger) and
the hopper's dedicated mechanical lockout switch.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Notify all machines connected to the DCS-800 (router, edge bander,
   sanders) that dust extraction is going offline before beginning
   isolation — connected machines must not be started during this
   lockout, per each machine's own manual.
2. Bring the fan and auger to a full stop using the normal control
   sequence.
3. Open and lock the main electrical disconnect with a personal lockout
   device and dated tag.
4. Engage the hopper's dedicated mechanical lockout switch before
   opening any hopper access door, per Safety Prerequisite §4 of the
   full manual — this is a separate, additional isolation point beyond
   the main electrical disconnect.
5. Verify isolation with a try-out test at the control panel before
   opening any access point.
6. On completing work, confirm all access doors are closed and the
   grounding/bonding straps are reconnected if disturbed, notify
   connected-machine operators, then remove lockout devices in reverse
   order.
