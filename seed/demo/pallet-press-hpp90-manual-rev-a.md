# Hydraulic Pallet Press HPP-90 — Maintenance & Operations Manual (Rev. A)

## Overview & Specifications

The HPP-90 is a small hydraulic press used at the Damietta facility to nail
and clamp softwood pallets. Rated force: 90 kN. Maximum working pressure:
160 bar. Hydraulic reservoir capacity: 40 litres of ISO VG 46 oil.

This is a demonstration document used in the README's five-minute demo path
to show ingestion of a new manual while the system is running.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Isolate electrical power at the local disconnect and apply your personal
   lock and tag before opening any guard on the press.
2. Release stored hydraulic pressure by operating the manual relief valve
   until the pressure gauge reads 0 bar, then wait 5 minutes before removing
   any hydraulic fitting.
3. Confirm the ram is resting on its mechanical safety block before placing
   any part of your body between the platens.

## Maintenance Schedule

Check the hydraulic oil level weekly and top up with ISO VG 46 oil if it is
below the MIN mark. Replace the return-line filter every 500 operating hours.
Inspect hoses and fittings monthly for weeping or abrasion.

## Diagnostic & Troubleshooting

1. Symptom: ram moves slowly or the press cannot reach full force. Likely
   cause: low oil level or a blocked suction strainer. Corrective action:
   check the reservoir level, inspect the suction strainer for debris, and
   top up with ISO VG 46 oil; do not run the pump dry.
2. Symptom: oil temperature above 60C during a shift. Likely cause: relief
   valve set too low or a clogged return filter. Corrective action: stop the
   press, allow the oil to cool for 20 minutes, and replace the return-line
   filter; escalate to a certified technician if the temperature recurs.
