# Industrial Air Compressor IAC-100 — Lockout/Tagout Card

## Overview & Specifications

This card is the mandatory lockout/tagout (LOTO) sequence for any
maintenance on the IAC-100 compressor, tank, or piping. Two isolation
points apply: the main electrical disconnect and stored tank pressure —
this is doubly important because the compressor can auto-start on a
pressure drop with no warning, per Safety Prerequisite §4 of the full
manual.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Notify affected personnel that the compressor is being locked out
   before beginning isolation.
2. Open and lock the main electrical disconnect with a personal lockout
   device and dated tag — this is the step that prevents an unexpected
   auto-start during maintenance.
3. Fully bleed tank pressure via the manual drain valve and confirm zero
   on the pressure gauge before opening any fitting, per Safety
   Prerequisite §1 of the full manual.
4. Verify isolation with a try-out test at the control panel (confirm no
   start is possible) before beginning work.
5. On completing work, confirm the belt guard is reinstalled and
   secured, notify affected personnel, then remove the lockout device.
