# Service Bulletin SB-IAC-100-01 — Premature Belt Tensioner Wear on Early Units

## Overview & Specifications

Applies to IAC-100 units with serial prefix IAC10-A only (units IAC10-B
onward use a revised tensioner design and are not affected). Field
reports indicate the original belt tensioner design can wear out
significantly faster than the standard weekly inspection interval would
catch, occasionally leading to belt slip between inspections.

## Diagnostic & Troubleshooting

1. Symptom: belt shows visible glazing or a burnt rubber smell despite
   passing the most recent weekly tension check. Likely cause: rapid
   tensioner wear on affected serials allowing tension to drop between
   inspections. Corrective action: for affected serial prefixes,
   increase belt tension checks to twice-weekly until the tensioner is
   replaced with the revised part; do not rely on the standard weekly
   interval alone for this serial range.
2. Symptom: replaced belt shows the same glazing pattern again within
   two months. Likely cause: the tensioner itself, not the belt, is the
   worn component. Corrective action: replace the tensioner assembly
   with the revised part number below rather than replacing the belt a
   second time.

## Parts Catalog

- Belt tensioner assembly, revised design — part no. IAC-BTA-02
