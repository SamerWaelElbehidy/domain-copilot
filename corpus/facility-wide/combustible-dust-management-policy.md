# Dawlia Furniture Works — Combustible Dust Management Policy

## Overview & Specifications

Fine wood dust generated by the CNC router, edge bander, and sanding
stations is a combustible dust hazard: an accumulated dust cloud can
explode if ignited. This policy applies facility-wide and works
alongside the Dust Extraction & Collection System DCS-800's own Safety
Prerequisites, which implement several of the controls listed here at
the equipment level.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. No accumulation of loose dust on horizontal surfaces (beams, ledges,
   equipment tops) beyond a light, easily-brushed layer — visible
   accumulated dust must be cleaned before the end of the shift in which
   it is noticed.
2. Housekeeping dust removal must use a vacuum system rated for
   combustible dust or wet-cleaning methods — compressed-air blow-down
   of accumulated dust is prohibited, as it can create an ignitable dust
   cloud.
3. No hot work (welding, grinding, open flame) anywhere in dust-
   generating or dust-collection areas without an issued hot-work permit
   and a dedicated fire watch, consistent with the DCS-800 manual's own
   Safety Prerequisite on this topic.
4. All dust-handling equipment (ducting, hoppers, collection bins) must
   maintain verified electrical grounding/bonding per the DCS-800
   manual's schedule — static discharge is a recognized ignition source
   for combustible dust.
5. Any equipment operator noticing unusual dust accumulation, a damaged
   duct, or a grounding fault must report it the same shift per the
   Incident & Near-Miss Reporting Procedure, not defer it to the next
   scheduled inspection.
