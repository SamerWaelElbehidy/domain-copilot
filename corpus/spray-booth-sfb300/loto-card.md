# Spray Finishing Booth SFB-300 — Lockout/Tagout Card

## Overview & Specifications

This card is the mandatory lockout/tagout (LOTO) sequence for
maintenance on the SFB-300's exhaust fan, filters, or explosion-proof
electrical fittings. One isolation point applies: the main electrical
disconnect, which de-energizes the exhaust fan and all booth-internal
electrical fittings together. The fire suppression system is not
isolated by this LOTO card — it must remain armed except during its own
certified service procedure, performed only by a certified technician.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Notify affected personnel and confirm no spraying is in progress
   before beginning isolation.
2. Allow booth airflow to clear any residual vapor for at least 5
   minutes after the last spray application before beginning isolation.
3. Open and lock the main electrical disconnect with a personal lockout
   device and dated tag.
4. Confirm no ignition sources are introduced into the booth during
   maintenance, consistent with Safety Prerequisite §2 of the full
   manual — isolating power does not remove the flammable-vapor hazard
   from residual overspray or filter media.
5. Verify isolation with a try-out test at the control panel before
   beginning work.
6. On completing work, confirm all guards and filters are reinstalled,
   notify affected personnel, then remove the lockout device.
