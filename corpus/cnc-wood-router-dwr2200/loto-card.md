# CNC Wood Router DWR-2200 — Lockout/Tagout Card

## Overview & Specifications

This card is the mandatory lockout/tagout (LOTO) sequence for any
maintenance, blade/bit change, or fault-clearing task on the DWR-2200.
It supplements, and does not replace, Safety Prerequisite §7 of the full
Operations & Maintenance Manual. Two isolation points apply: the main
electrical disconnect and the spindle's residual rotational energy.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Notify all operators and nearby personnel that the DWR-2200 is being
   locked out for maintenance before touching any control.
2. Bring the spindle to a full stop using the normal control-panel stop
   sequence; do not proceed to isolation while the spindle is still
   decelerating.
3. Open and lock the main electrical disconnect with a personal lockout
   device and attach a dated tag identifying the person performing the
   lockout.
4. Wait a minimum of 30 seconds after electrical isolation before
   touching the spindle housing — the spindle can retain rotational
   energy briefly after a software stop, per the full manual's Safety
   Prerequisite §7.
5. Before beginning work, attempt to start the machine from the control
   panel (try-out test) to verify the lockout is effective; return the
   control to the off position immediately after the test.
6. On completing work, remove tools, confirm all guards are reinstalled,
   notify affected personnel, and only then remove the lockout device
   and tag.
