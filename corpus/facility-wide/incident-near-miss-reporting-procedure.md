# Dawlia Furniture Works — Incident & Near-Miss Reporting Procedure

## Overview & Specifications

This procedure applies to any injury, property damage, or near-miss
(an event that could have caused injury or damage but did not) anywhere
in the Damietta facility. The Rev. C update to the CNC Wood Router
DWR-2200 manual, adding a dust-hose safety check, was issued as a direct
result of a near-miss reported under this procedure.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Report any incident or near-miss to the shift supervisor before the
   end of the current shift, regardless of severity — a near-miss with
   no injury or damage is still reportable and required.
2. Do not disturb the scene of an incident involving injury or
   significant damage until the supervisor has documented it, unless
   disturbing it is necessary to prevent further harm.
3. The shift supervisor must file a written report within 24 hours,
   including what happened, the machine or area involved, and any
   immediate corrective action taken.
4. Any report involving a machine's safety system (guard, interlock,
   E-stop, LOTO point) must be escalated to the equipment's
   manufacturer-liaison technician for review, in addition to internal
   reporting.
5. Corrective actions arising from an incident report (including manual
   revisions) must be tracked to completion and referenced in the
   affected equipment's own Revision History once implemented.
