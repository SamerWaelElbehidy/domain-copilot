# Hydraulic Laminating Press HLP-1200 — Lockout/Tagout Card

## Overview & Specifications

This card is the mandatory lockout/tagout (LOTO) sequence for any
maintenance inside the platen zone of the HLP-1200. Three isolation
points apply: the main electrical disconnect, stored hydraulic pressure,
and residual platen heat.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. Notify affected personnel that the press is being locked out before
   beginning isolation.
2. Bring the press to the fully open position using the normal control
   sequence before isolating power.
3. Open and lock the main electrical disconnect with a personal lockout
   device and dated tag.
4. Release full hydraulic system pressure via the manual bleed valve and
   confirm zero on the pressure gauge, consistent with Safety
   Prerequisite §4 of the full manual.
5. Engage the mechanical safety props under the upper platen before any
   part of the body enters the platen zone — hydraulic pressure release
   alone is not sufficient protection against platen drop.
6. Confirm platen surface temperature reads below 40C before contact, or
   wear heat-resistant gloves if work must begin sooner.
7. Verify isolation with a try-out test (attempt the two-hand start
   sequence; confirm no motion) before beginning work inside the zone.
8. On completing work, remove tools and props, confirm guards are
   reinstalled, notify affected personnel, then remove lockout devices.
