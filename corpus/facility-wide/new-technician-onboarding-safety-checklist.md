# Dawlia Furniture Works — New Technician Onboarding Safety Checklist

## Overview & Specifications

This checklist must be completed and signed off before a new technician
is authorized to operate any equipment unsupervised. It applies in
addition to any machine-specific training sign-off referenced in the
General Workshop Safety Policy.

## Safety Prerequisites

1. New technician has read and acknowledged the General Workshop Safety
   Policy and the PPE Standard before entering production areas.
2. New technician has been walked through the facility's emergency exit
   routes, fire assembly point, and the location of the nearest fire
   extinguisher and first-aid station.
3. New technician has observed a full shift on each machine they will
   operate before receiving hands-on training on that machine.
4. New technician has performed the lockout-tagout sequence for each
   machine they will operate, under direct supervision, per the
   Company-wide Lockout-Tagout Standard and that machine's own LOTO
   card.
5. New technician has been briefed on the Incident & Near-Miss Reporting
   Procedure and understands reporting is required the same shift.
6. Supervisor has signed off on each machine the new technician is
   authorized to operate unsupervised; the technician must not operate
   any machine not listed on this sign-off.
